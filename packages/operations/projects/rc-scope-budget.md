Matt's synthesis of Regen Coordination's **core functions**, aligned with a concrete budget for a timeboxed council mandate. Answers two questions the council has circled since April: *what does RC actually do?* and *how do we pay for it with the treasury we have?*

**Governance approach**: council works for a predetermined timeframe (rest of Q2 + Q3), documents commitments/outputs/outcomes, then reviews and reassesses — ideally with a much more open and democratic decision process after that (bring more people into the council, or decentralize it entirely).
