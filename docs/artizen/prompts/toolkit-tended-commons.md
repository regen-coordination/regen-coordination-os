# Artifact prompt — "The Tended Commons" (TOOLKIT / KNOWLEDGE COMMONS, direction C)

Third direction, and the most **literal** reading of what the Toolkit says it is:
*"a regenerative web3 knowledge garden that doubles as a design and deployment framework"*
(`repos/regen-toolkit/SOUL.md`).

**How this differs from the committed Toolkit artifact.**
[`toolkit-knowledge-garden-layers-CHOSEN-2026-08-05.png`](toolkit-knowledge-garden-layers-CHOSEN-2026-08-05.png)
argues the *architecture* — a cross-section, strata, a network binding layers. It is a diagram of
structure. This one argues the *commons*: cultivated ground divided into many distinct tended
plots, worked by many hands, linked by paths that carry light between them. Structure vs.
stewardship. Both are true of the Toolkit; they are not the same claim.

Layer 2 of the architecture is literally **Knowledge Commons / Encyclopedia** (Heenal, `active` —
the most mature layer per `docs/LAYERS.md`), so a commons reading is not a metaphor stretched over
the project. It is the layer doing the most work.

> As with every prompt in this set, **no count is hard-coded.** "Many plots," never a number —
> the article inventory and learning-path counts are exactly the figures `HANDOFF.md` §6 flags as
> unverified against the live site.

Reference is RC's shipped artifact for the flat editorial vector treatment (this is a landscape,
not a cross-section). Palette per [`docs/DESIGN.md`](../../DESIGN.md) §2.1: `#aab8c1` → `#d4c8b8`

---

Create a new image in exactly the visual style of the reference image: a flat, modern editorial
vector illustration with clean shapes, soft luminous glows, a fine film-grain texture over the
whole image, and a warm graduated sky.

The scene: a broad cultivated hillside at dawn, seen from a raised three-quarter vantage so the
ground plane opens out across most of the frame. The horizon sits high, in the upper quarter. The
sky above graduates from a cool slate-blue (#aab8c1) at the top, through a pale warm sand
(#d4c8b8), into a soft yellow-green (#e3dd91) and then a warm sand-orange (#e0b789) where the sun
is rising.

The hillside is divided into **many distinct cultivated plots** — irregular terraced beds of
varying size and shape, following the contours of the slope. Each plot is planted differently:
some in rows of low green crops, some in taller grasses, some freshly turned earth in warm
sand-orange and amber-gold, some in flowering ground cover. No two plots read the same. They fit
together like a patchwork that grew rather than one that was drawn.

Running along every path between the plots: **fine lines of soft white light**, following the
boundaries and terrace edges, with small luminous nodes wherever paths cross. The light forms one
continuous connected network across the entire hillside — every plot touched, none isolated. The
lines are brightest where several paths meet.

A few slender young trees stand at path junctions. In the sky above, a faint scattering of small
luminous points echoes the pattern of the plots below, as if the ground's arrangement were
reflected upward.

Mood: tended, shared, patient, orderly without being uniform. Ground that many hands work.

Absolutely no text, no letters, no numbers, no logos, no watermarks, no buildings, no fences, no
vehicles, no human figures, no tools.

Square composition, high detail, smooth gradients, fine grain texture.
