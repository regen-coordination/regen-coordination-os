# Artifact prompt — "Confluence of Streams" (FUND, direction B)

Second prototype direction for the **Regen Coordination Fund** entry. Same brief as
[`fund-constellation-of-groves`](fund-constellation-of-groves.md); different argument about what a
fund *is*.

**The idea.** Direction A says a fund is *many things held together*. This one says a fund is
**many flows pooling into one** — tributaries of light running down through the valley and
converging into a single luminous river. That is closer to what the Fund mechanically does:
capital from several sources gathers, then routes onward to curated projects.

It also keeps RC's own landscape and the river that already appears in the April artifact, so the
lineage is visible without repeating the suspended-lattice motif twice across two entries.

> **Which direction wins is a brand call, not a gate call.** Both should clear §5; they differ in
> what they *claim*. A reads as a commons of peers, B reads as pooled capital with direction. If
> the Fund's story to funders is "we curate a set," A is truer; if it is "we aggregate and route,"
> B is.

Palette per [`docs/DESIGN.md`](../../DESIGN.md) §2.1: `--brand-sky #aab8c1` → `--brand-horizon
#d4c8b8` → `--brand-pasture #e3dd91` → `--brand-sun #e0b789` → `--brand-sun-deep #d69f44`.

---

Create a new image in exactly the visual style of the reference image: a flat, modern editorial
vector illustration with clean shapes, soft luminous glows, a fine film-grain texture over the
whole image, and a warm graduated sky.

The scene: a wide valley at dawn seen from a high vantage point, looking down its length. Rolling
forested hills in layered greens step down both sides toward the valley floor, receding into soft
atmospheric haze. The horizon sits high in the upper third of the frame. The sky above graduates
from a cool slate-blue (#aab8c1) at the top, through a pale warm sand (#d4c8b8), into a soft
yellow-green (#e3dd91) and then a warm sand-orange (#e0b789) at the horizon where the sun is
rising.

Running down the hillsides: **several separate streams of soft white light**, each descending from
a different fold in the hills, varying in width and brightness, each threaded with small luminous
nodes along its length like beads on a line. They wind downward and **converge toward the centre
of the valley floor**, joining one after another into a single broad luminous river that flows
toward the viewer through the foreground.

The joined river is the brightest element in the image, warm gold (#d69f44) at its centre, cooling
to pale white at its edges. Where two streams meet, the light pools and brightens.

The streams are part of the land, not floating above it — they read as water carrying light.

Mood: gathering, purposeful, calm, abundant. Many sources becoming one current.

Absolutely no text, no letters, no numbers, no logos, no watermarks, no buildings, no vehicles,
no human figures, no boats.

Square composition, high detail, smooth gradients, fine grain texture.
